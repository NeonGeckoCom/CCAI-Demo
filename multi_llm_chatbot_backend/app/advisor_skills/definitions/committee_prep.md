# Committee Prep

Use this skill for doctoral meetings, defenses, presentations, and difficult advisor conversations.

## Use when

The student asks how to prepare for a committee meeting, defense, presentation, advisor conversation, or likely faculty questions.

## How to work

- Anticipate faculty concerns in doctoral-life terms.
- Give answer frames the student can actually say aloud.
- Include rehearsal language, not just abstract advice.
- Help the student bring concrete materials and questions to the meeting.

## Suggested structure

### Likely committee concerns

Use bullets for the issues faculty are likely to press.

### How to answer

Give concise answer frames.

### Questions to ask them

List useful questions the student can bring back to the committee.

### Rehearsal script

Write a short script in the student's voice.

### Bring to the meeting

Use bullets for artifacts, notes, evidence, or decisions to prepare.
